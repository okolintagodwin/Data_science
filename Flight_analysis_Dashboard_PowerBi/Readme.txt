Setting
A Data Scientist working for the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey.
The Port Authority is, among other things, in charge of operating 5 airports in the New
York area. The task is to create a prototype of an interactive dashboard to visualize
patterns in flights departing from these airports.

For the prototype I will use 6 months of flights data from the Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) for the year 2024, along with the corresponding metadata on
airports (e.g. John F Kennedy International Airport), aircrafts (AIRBUS A321), and
airlines (American Airlines).

The Port Authority
• is particularly interested in numbers and patterns related to departure delays or
cancellations,
• is also open for insights into other aspects that you identify as interesting,
• asks you to allow for some level of user interaction, such as filtering by airport or
airline,
• asks you do not overwhelm the user with too much information, but display just
those aspects that you consider most interesting.
